Pod restarts & deaths

Pod lifecycle events from Kubernetes (the k8s/events stream). A restart is an in-place container restart — most often an out-of-memory kill the kernel didn't log to us — so a pod whose work stops mid-visit with no traceback usually has a restart here. Each row is attributed to the dataId the pod was processing at that moment; click it to open that visit.
