Pod restarts & deaths

Pod lifecycle events from Kubernetes (the k8s/events stream). A restart is an in-place container restart — most often an out-of-memory kill the kernel didn't log to us — so a pod whose work stops mid-visit with no traceback usually has a restart here. Each row is attributed to the dataId the pod was processing at that moment; click it to open that visit.

What am I looking at?

What the rapid analysis pipeline did for each image, reconstructed from its logs.

  • Tonight — exposures as they are taken; view appears once an exposure's logs are ready (~5 min after shutter close).
  • Exposure view — one exposure's processing. Each row is a pipeline worker, coloured bars are its tasks, and time runs from that exposure's shutter close (t0 = 0). Click a row for its full log.
  • Night view — a whole night at a glance: how long processing took per image, and every failure with a click-through to its traceback.

Times are UTC. A red banner means some logs could not be retrieved, so that view may be incomplete.
